01 / ABOUT

Engineering from concept to hardware.

I started in Biological Systems Engineering at UC Davis, where I designed a pilot-scale mycoprotein plant and built a solar food dehydrator out of plywood. Then I spent two years as a process engineer at California Safe Soils, keeping production equipment running and developing Cal-Plex, a liquid calcium fertilizer.

Now I’m finishing my M.S. in Bioengineering at San Diego State, fabricating microsupercapacitors in a cleanroom. What ties it all together is that I like taking a problem from first calculations and CAD all the way through fabrication, testing, and iteration — and I want to keep doing that in design and microfabrication engineering.
